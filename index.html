C-44 Kestrel Cockpit Guide

Panel Operation (Mouse)

  • ENG Master: Flip ON to start turbine idle before pushing throttle.
  • Throttle & Spoiler Levers: Click or drag levers up/down (or scroll wheel while hovering). Above 80% lights afterburner boost.
  • Pitch Lever & ALT HOLD: Drag PITCH to set climb/descent angle. Click 0° LVL to center or ALT HOLD to cruise level hands-free.
  • Flaps, Gear & Brakes: Use T/O flaps for takeoff, retract GEAR and FLAPS in cruise to save fuel, and pre-arm BRAKES + LAND flaps + GEAR DN before landing.

Route & Refuel Procedure

  • Takeoff: Rotate pitch to +10°..+15° once airspeed passes 25 m/s.
  • Midway Stop (MESA-4): Touch down under 7.6 m/s sink rate on the elevated mesa strip, come to a stop, and click REFUEL PUMP.
  • Final Leg (SOLIS): Climb over Cloudspine Ridge (332 m), descend along the green coastal glide slope, and stop on the runway.
